FoodMacro Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

FoodMacro ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the FoodMacro mobile application.

1. Information You Provide

FoodMacro allows you to enter and track nutrition information, including meals, calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat.

You may also provide photographs of food when using the AI Food Scanner feature.

2. Food Photos and AI Analysis

When you use the AI Food Scanner, the photograph you select or take may be transmitted to our server for the purpose of analyzing the visible food and estimating nutritional information.

The AI analysis may identify foods, estimate portions, and estimate calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat.

FoodMacro nutrition estimates are informational only and may not always be accurate. You should review and verify nutritional information before relying on it.

3. Local Storage

FoodMacro stores certain information locally on your device, including meals and nutrition goals, so that the app can remember your information between sessions.

4. Third-Party Services

FoodMacro may use third-party technology and services to provide AI-powered food analysis and related functionality.

Food photographs submitted for AI analysis may be processed by third-party AI service providers used by FoodMacro to provide the requested analysis.

5. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

FoodMacro does not require you to create an account to use the current version of the application.

We do not intentionally collect your name, home address, telephone number, or financial information through the current version of FoodMacro.

6. Data Retention

Nutrition information stored locally by FoodMacro remains on your device until you delete it or remove the application, subject to the behavior of your device and operating system.

Food images submitted for AI analysis are transmitted for the purpose of providing the requested analysis. We do not intentionally use submitted food photographs for advertising or to sell personal information.

7. Children's Privacy

FoodMacro is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the app. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or FoodMacro, please contact us through the support information provided for FoodMacro.
